How to be green

What is a green person by the way? In short, a green person is some who takes care of the planet or the environment and has a lifestyle that is environmentally and eco-friendly.

How to be green? That question has been answered thousands of times already and there are thousands of articles on the internet talking about it, that means that the practices that we can take in order to be Green are well known for most of us. However, in this article we are going to do a review of some of those habits and practices despite the fact what I said, because that’s the point: engrave on the people’s heads how to take care of the planet with actions that everyone can do.

How to go green?

If you want to go green I'd suggest you to read more article apart from this, as I said at the beginning: there are thousands of articles talking about this topic. So what you should do is to read as much you can about the topic!!

Go to google and try to search what are the problems that affects the environment, what cause them, why are them a problem, what are the solutions, how can we take care of environment etc. Read the much as you can and this way you can decide what changes can you do in your life to take care of the plant and to be part of the solution of some of the problems that affect it.

That’s the way to go green.

Some habits and practices you can adopt to go green:

 

1) Turn off the lights

Let’s start with a classic: when the lights are not being used or you leave a room and you left nobody there, turn the lights off! That’s the first step, the second one is to try using the natural light whenever you can’t instead of the light bulb, so, open your windows!

But, why? By doing that you save electricity. But how does saving electricity contribute positively to the environment? You might be wondering, well: “When you consume less power, you reduce the amount of toxic fumes released by power plants, conserve the earth’s natural resources and protect ecosystems from destruction.”

2) Reduce your energy consumption

This is like the first tip, but applying it for more things in general. You can reduce your energy consumption by turning off any stuff that needs electricity (I mean electric appliances or home appliances) that don’t need to be on and are not being used. For example: If nobody is watching the TV, you should turn it off, or if you have a desktop computer don’t let it on all night if isn’t necessary. This way you save energy.

Adding more to what I said early about saving electricity, if everyone reduces their energy consumption the plants that provide us of energy have to work less to satisfy the demand of energy and this way reducing the pollution that those plants release to the environment; “To generate electricity, most power plants burn coal, crude oil or other fossil fuels. Although this method of creating energy is relatively inexpensive, our planet pays the price – carbon dioxide, sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides are just a few of the byproducts that come from traditional methods of power generation.”

3) Reuse

We Dominicans are pretty good at this one, reusing, you can reuse a lot of things, I won’t explain what reuse is because at this point I assume everyone know what it is.

But why reusing is important? Well, it prevents pollution, because its reduce the need to get more material to make any product and reduce the amount of a product that has to be manufactured thus saving energy (remember what we talked above) and also factories generate many gases and leave residues that pollute the environment.

Did you know that reusing is more easy to do than recycle? Yes, Reusing is even more viable or better than recycling because it saves the energy that comes with having to dismantle and re-manufacture products that recycling require.

4) Plant a tree

Yes, if you want to be a green person you should plant trees or plants, because after all the “green” word in this context was choose because the nature is green!! (because of the trees and plants). Trees and plants give off oxygen that we need to breathe (Btw the 50-70% comes from ocean), they absorb carbon dioxide, provide food and habitat for wildlife and humans, and regulate the water cycle. They are extremely important for the planet!!! So at least try to plant or have some plants at home. It worth to mention that a lot of people already do this, so we are on the good way with this one.

That’s it for today, it only remains to say that don’t just keep reading and getting information, also go and put in practice what you’ve learned and what you will learn!!

Thanks for reading!!
